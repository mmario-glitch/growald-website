2. General information

We process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide this website, to handle inquiries, or to carry out pre-contractual and contractual measures. Personal data is any information that can be used to identify you personally.

3. Hosting and server log data

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When the website is accessed, technically necessary connection data may be processed, in particular IP address, browser and device information, time of access and the page accessed. Among other purposes, GitHub processes and logs visitors' IP addresses for security reasons.

Processing is based on our legitimate interest in a secure, stable and technically error-free provision of the website pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Further information can be found in the GitHub privacy policy.

4. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the information you provide in order to handle your inquiry and for possible follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where pre-contractual or contractual measures are concerned, and otherwise our legitimate interest in handling your inquiry pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Emails are stored with our email hosting provider, which processes them on our behalf as a processor. To handle inquiries, we may use AI-based tools whose providers are likewise bound as processors; no automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

We store this data only for as long as is necessary for the respective purpose and statutory retention obligations.

5. Appointment booking via Calendly

We use Calendly (Calendly LLC, USA) for appointment bookings. Our website contains only a link to Calendly; no Calendly content is loaded merely by visiting our website. If you book an appointment via the link, we process the information you provide in the booking form (in particular name, email address, your answers and the selected time) in order to arrange and conduct the appointment. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

Calendly processes this booking data on our behalf as a processor on the basis of a data processing agreement. Calendly is independently responsible for the visit to the Calendly page itself (e.g. cookies set there); for this, see the Calendly privacy notice. We store booking data for as long as necessary to handle the appointment and possible follow-up conversations, and delete it afterwards unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

On this website, we do not use any analytics, tracking or advertising services ourselves and do not store any cookies of our own. External sites that you reach via links may use their own technologies; their privacy notices apply to those.

GitHub (hosting) and Calendly (appointment booking) are based in the USA. Both providers are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the adequacy decision of the European Commission of July 10, 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, the providers have agreed to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Details can be found in the privacy notices of the respective providers.
