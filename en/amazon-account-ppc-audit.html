Amazon account & PPC audit

A complete check of Account Health, listings and ad account, as the basis for all further Amazon measures.

Where the account loses money.

Many Amazon accounts grow historically without anyone reviewing them systematically.

  • It is unclear where the account is actually burning money right now
  • Account Health, listings and PPC are viewed in isolation instead of as a system
  • Warnings or performance problems are noticed late
  • There is no structured starting point for optimization
  • Agencies or internal teams change without the status being documented
