Reporting & business intelligence dashboards

Bring data sources together and build a clear KPI cockpit, instead of spreadsheet sprawl and unused Power BI dashboards.

Where decisions are made today without a data foundation.

Data is often scattered across Excel, CRM, ERP and email.

  • Investments in tools like Power BI often lead to overloaded, unused dashboards
  • There is no single cockpit with the most important metrics for decisions
  • Decisions are made on gut feeling instead of data
  • Maintaining existing dashboards ties up a disproportionate amount of resources
  • Different departments work with different, contradictory numbers

Frequently asked questions.

Do we have to replace Power BI?

Not necessarily. We check whether a lean custom solution or an optimization of your existing tools makes more sense.

How many metrics should a good dashboard show?

A few top-level KPIs up front, with the option to click deeper, not everything at once.

Who maintains the dashboard afterwards?

This is clarified in advance: internal, external or shared, depending on the resources available.

Can several departments get their own views?

Yes, different roles can access the same data foundation with their own views.

How quickly is a first cockpit usable?

A first draft with the most important KPIs is usually created within the 20-hour starter package.
