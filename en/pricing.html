What does an Amazon agency cost?

At growald, Amazon consulting costs €89/hr net, or €1,780 net for the 20-hour starter package. For ongoing management there are three monthly packages at €1,500, €3,500 and €10,000 net per month, each with a 6-month minimum term. We work out which model fits in a free growth analysis first.

Is there a minimum term?

Not for individual consulting hours. The monthly Amazon management packages run for at least 6 months.

What is not included in the price?

Ad budget, goods, logistics and platform costs. For the Growthteam package, a fee of 25% of total ad spend applies from €10,000 ad spend.

What does the intro call cost?

Nothing. The growth analysis takes 30 minutes, is free and does not commit you to any booking.
