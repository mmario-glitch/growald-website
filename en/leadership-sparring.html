Sparring for management and e-commerce leadership

An experienced sounding board for strategic e-commerce decisions, without the blind spots of day-to-day operations.

When important decisions stay within your own circle.

Strategic decisions sometimes need an outside perspective.

Frequently asked questions.

How often does the sparring take place?

As needed, from weekly to occasion-based for specific decisions.

No, it complements internal bodies with an external, operational e-commerce perspective.

What company size is this suited to?

Mainly established brands from around one million euros in annual revenue.

Yes, the focus is agreed with you individually.
