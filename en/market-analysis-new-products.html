Market, keyword & competitor analysis for new products

Before capital goes into a new product: assess demand, competition and market potential reliably.

When product ideas come from gut feeling.

Many new products are launched before demand has really been checked.

  • New product ideas often come from gut feeling instead of market data
  • Search volume and actual purchase demand get mixed up
  • Competitive density and price level only become visible after launch
  • Misjudgments tie up capital in products without real demand
  • There is no repeatable method for assessing new ideas

Frequently asked questions.

Which channels is demand checked on?

Usually Amazon and Google, depending on where your target group actually searches.

Is high search volume enough to base a decision on?

No, search volume is only one factor alongside competitive density, price level and margin.

How many product ideas can be checked at the same time?

Usually two to four, depending on the scope of the starter package.

What happens after a "Go" recommendation?

Calculation and the business case follow, then sourcing and launch planning.
