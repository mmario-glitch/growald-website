Shopify consulting

Shop setup, optimization and processes around Shopify, hands-on rather than just theoretical.

When the shop falls short of its potential.

Many Shopify shops are running without anyone tapping their full potential.

  • The shop is set up, but structure, load time or conversion fall short of their potential
  • Apps pile up without anyone keeping track of their value and cost
  • Checkout, product pages or navigation lose customers at unnecessary points
  • Processes between shop, fulfillment and bookkeeping run manually instead of automatically
  • There is no clear plan for which Shopify features make sense next

Shop and marketplaces working together

Shopify optimization is not looked at in isolation, but together with Amazon and other marketplaces, so that product range, prices and processes stay consistent.

Result: a shop that loads faster, loses fewer customers at checkout and is cleanly connected to fulfillment and bookkeeping.
