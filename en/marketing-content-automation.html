Marketing & content automation for businesses

Create SEO copy, product descriptions and social media content at scale instead of writing piece by piece by hand.

Where content creation hits its limits today.

In many companies, content is created ad hoc instead of to a plan.

  • Blog articles and campaigns sit for months because there is no time
  • SEO copy, product descriptions and social media posts take a lot of time per piece
  • With many products or SKUs, manual copywriting does not scale
  • No consistent brand voice across different channels
  • Keyword research and content creation are treated separately instead of together
