CRM setup & CRM automation for companies

From spreadsheet sprawl to a central, automated CRM with an overview of your entire sales pipeline.

Where customer data gets lost today.

Many companies with considerable revenue have no working CRM system.

  • Customer data scattered across spreadsheets, email inboxes and notes
  • No overview of the sales pipeline and open opportunities
  • Standard software such as Microsoft Dynamics is often too expensive or too complex for the actual need
  • Customer follow-ups are forgotten or delayed
  • No link between the CRM and other processes such as quotes or invoices

Frequently asked questions.

Do we absolutely need a new CRM tool?

No, we first check whether an existing system can be sensibly extended.

What happens to our existing spreadsheet data?

It is migrated in a structured way, nothing gets lost.

Does a custom-built CRM make more sense than standard software?

It depends on the requirements. For very specific processes, a custom build can be cheaper and a better fit.

How long does the rollout take?

It depends on the scope. A first working core is usually built within the 20-hour starter package.

Can several employees work at the same time?

Yes, that is a basic requirement for a working CRM.
