A good ROAS can hide a bad business.

Managing Amazon by ACoS and ROAS alone often overlooks margin, organic impact, and the quality of the product detail page.

  • Campaigns grow, but contribution margin and break-even ACoS are not clearly defined
  • Search terms, bids, and campaign structures are optimized without clear hypotheses
  • Listings and content limit conversion while more ad budget is spent
  • Organic revenue share, TACoS, and availability are looked at separately from each other
  • Reporting describes numbers but delivers no prioritized decisions

Frequently asked questions about Amazon consulting.

Does growald handle only PPC or also listings and content?

Both are possible. We treat advertising, retail readiness, listing SEO, content, conversion, availability, and contribution margin as one connected system.

Which metrics are looked at?

Depending on the goal, among others ACoS, break-even ACoS, TACoS, ROAS, contribution margin, conversion, organic revenue share, availability, and search terms.

Can we start with an audit?

Yes. A focused audit evaluates campaigns, listings, profitability, and account structure and turns the findings into a prioritized action list.

Is there a revenue or ROAS guarantee?

No. Results depend, among other things, on product, price, margin, competition, availability, content, and ad budget. We promise traceable work and clear decisions.

How does the collaboration begin?

With a free growth analysis. After that, a 20-hour package, an audit, or, if it fits your needs, ongoing support can follow.

Does growald also offer legal advice?

No, growald does not offer legal advice. For legal topics (e.g., contracts, vendor/seller terms, compliance requirements), we recommend consulting a lawyer or tax advisor. We support you operationally and strategically.
