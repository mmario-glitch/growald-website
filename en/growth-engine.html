Growth Engine: Amazon ads, UGC and AI in one package

Monthly support from a single source: Amazon advertising, creator videos and AI automation that work together.

When advertising, content and processes run separately.

Growth on Amazon rarely fails because of a single lever, but because the levers do not work together.

  • Ads, content and processes sit with different service providers
  • Campaigns run without fresh video material and wear out
  • Advertising is optimized for revenue instead of contribution margin
  • Routine work such as reporting and content upkeep eats up team time
  • There is no shared view of goals and results

Frequently asked questions.

What does the Growth Engine package cost?

The price depends on advertising budget, number of products, videos and automations. After the intro call you receive an individual quote. Our monthly packages from €1,500 net serve as orientation.

How long is the minimum term?

Six months. In our experience, shorter periods are not enough to build and test advertising and content properly.

Is there a revenue guarantee?

No. We work with care and clear goals, but a particular revenue or a particular ranking is not guaranteed.

How many UGC videos are included?

We set that in the quote. Single videos cost from €150 net.

Are Meta or TikTok ads included too?

The focus is on Amazon. Other advertising channels are not part of the package.
