B2B retailer search for consumer brands

Find, evaluate, and approach suitable retailers, wholesalers, and distributors for your brand in a structured way.

When retail only grows through chance contacts.

Many brands sell well online but rarely find new retailers systematically.

  • New retailers only come through trade fairs and chance contacts
  • Unclear which retailers really fit your product range and price level
  • No time for systematic research and consistent follow-up
  • Outreach without a clear retailer benefit such as margin and terms fizzles out
  • Contacts and conversation status are scattered across inboxes

Here's how we work.

01

Target profile

Define retailer types, regions, terms, and minimum quantities together.

02

Research

Find suitable retailers and evaluate them by product range, size, and region.

03

Outreach

Approach retailers on behalf of your brand and follow up, with texts and channels agreed with you beforehand.

04

Handover

Retailers who show interest are handed over to you with a conversation note.

What is included.

Target retailer profile and selection criteria
Research and evaluation of suitable retailers
Retailer pitch with terms overview
Outreach and follow-up on behalf of your brand
Documentation of all contacts in one list
Handover of interested retailers to you

No guaranteed appointment, no guaranteed deal

We handle research and outreach carefully. Whether a retailer replies, books an appointment, or orders is up to the retailer. What we owe is the agreed work, not a specific result.

Result: An evaluated retailer list, documented outreach, and the handover of all retailers who show interest.

Frequently asked questions.

Which retailers do you approach?

Specialist retailers, wholesalers, and distributors that fit your product range and price level. We define regions and retailer types together.

Are appointments or orders guaranteed?

No. We handle research, outreach, and follow-up. Whether a retailer shows interest is up to the retailer.

On whose behalf is the outreach done?

On behalf of your brand. We agree the target list, texts, and channels with you beforehand.

Who negotiates the terms?

Usually you do. If you like, we prepare the terms overview and the conversation with you.

What does the retailer search cost?

You start with the 20-hour package at €1,780 net. After that, you decide whether and to what extent to continue.
