Launch planning for shop & marketplaces

Launch a new product without improvising listing, inventory, advertising and content at the last minute.

When the launch date becomes a test of nerves.

Many launches fail not because of the product, but because of coordination.

  • Launch dates slip because individual components are not ready in time
  • Listing, images, advertising and inventory are not planned in sync
  • Responsibilities between internal teams and external partners are unclear
  • The first impression at launch often decides visibility and reviews
  • After the launch, there is no clear plan for the first weeks
