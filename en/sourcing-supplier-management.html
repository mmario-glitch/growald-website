Sourcing and supplier management (RFQ, quote comparison)

From supplier search to inquiry to quote comparison, structured instead of random.

When supplier search stays a matter of chance.

Many supplier decisions happen by chance instead of systematically.

  • Supplier search often relies on chance finds instead of systematic research
  • Inquiries are imprecise, so quotes are hard to compare
  • Terms, payment targets and minimum order quantities are negotiated late
  • Quality and compliance requirements are missing from the inquiry
  • Without a comparison matrix, the supplier decision stays opaque

How we find and compare suppliers.

Frequently asked questions.

Is sourcing limited to China?

No, the methodology works for any sourcing market, but China is the most common case.

How many suppliers are compared?

Usually three to five, depending on product complexity.

Does growald handle the contract negotiation?

growald supports you with terms and comparison; you conclude legally binding contracts yourself.

What is an RFQ?

Request for quotation: a structured inquiry with clear specifications for comparable quotes.

Is a legal review of supply contracts included?

No, growald does not provide legal advice. For contract review we recommend a specialized lawyer.
