Calculation & business case (target price, landed cost)

From product idea to a reliable number: landed cost, target price, margin and break-even properly calculated.

When calculations are too optimistic.

Many calculations only consider the purchase price, not the full costs.

  • Calculations often only consider the purchase price, not the full landed cost
  • Customs, freight, fulfillment and advertising costs are underestimated
  • The target price is guessed rather than derived from market and margin
  • Break-even point and minimum sales volume are unknown before launch
  • Investment decisions are made without a reliable business case

Frequently asked questions.

Isn't a rough calculation enough?

Rough calculations often overlook freight, customs or fulfillment costs and distort the actual margin significantly.

Are advertising costs included too?

Yes, a realistic share of advertising costs is part of the business case.

What if the business case turns out negative?

Then that is a valid result, better recognized before committing capital than after.

Does the calculation only apply to Amazon?

No, it works across channels, for your shop and marketplaces.

Can the calculation be run for several scenarios?

Yes, best/realistic/worst-case scenarios are the norm.
