Frequently asked questions about e-commerce consulting.

Which companies is the consulting suited for?

The focus is on established consumer brands, manufacturers and retailers in the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) with their own shop and/or marketplace business. As a guide, annual revenue of roughly one million euros or more.

Do we have to be selling on Amazon already?

No. Amazon is only included if the channel makes sense for your goal and business case.

What do we get after the analysis?

A prioritized assessment of the most important levers, concrete next steps and, where it makes sense, a clearly defined work package.

Is the 20-hour package a fixed-price project?

No. It comprises 20 consulting hours at €89 net per hour. There is no guarantee of completion; progress and the next decision are documented transparently.

Does growald also support product development and sourcing?

Yes, from market validation and calculation through supplier search and samples to quality criteria, compliance plan and launch preparation.

Does growald also offer legal advice?

No, growald does not provide legal advice. For legal matters (e.g. contracts, compliance requirements) we recommend coordinating with a lawyer or tax advisor; we support you operationally and strategically.
