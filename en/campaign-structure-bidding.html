Campaign structure, bids & search terms

A campaign structure that stays understandable, with clean bid logic and controlled search terms.

When nobody understands the structure anymore.

Many campaign structures have grown over time and are hard to follow.

  • Automatic and manual campaigns overlap uncontrollably
  • Negative keywords are missing, wasted spend goes unnoticed
  • Bids are set across the board instead of by search term performance
  • New search terms are not systematically taken from search term reports
  • The structure grows over time and nobody understands the original logic anymore

Frequently asked questions.

How often should the structure be revised?

A basic framework usually lasts months, while ongoing maintenance (negative keywords, new search terms) should happen weekly.

What is a search term report?

The Amazon report that shows which actual search terms generated clicks and sales.

Is Amazon Ads software used too?

Depending on account size, additional software can make sense, but it is not a must.

Does this also apply to new products without history?

Yes, with an adapted starting structure for the launch phase.

How does this relate to ACoS/TACoS?

The structure is the foundation. Only afterwards can you sensibly optimize for break-even ACoS and TACoS.
