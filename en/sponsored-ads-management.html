Sponsored Products, Brands & Display

Build and manage Amazon ad accounts that optimize for contribution margin instead of just revenue.

When ad spend grows faster than profit.

Many ad accounts optimize for revenue, not for contribution margin.

  • Ad spend rises faster than actual profit
  • Sponsored Products, Brands and Display are not thought of as working together
  • The campaign structure has grown over the years and is hard to keep track of
  • Automatic and manual campaigns compete for the same search terms
  • Reporting shows ACoS, but no connection to actual margin
