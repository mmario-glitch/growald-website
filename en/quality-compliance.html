Quality assurance & compliance preparation

Organize samples, quality criteria and compliance preparation in a structured way, before a product goes to market.

When quality and compliance come too late.

Compliance issues are often discovered late in the process.

  • Samples are checked, but without clear, documented criteria
  • Compliance requirements (e.g. CE, packaging, labeling) are discovered late
  • Certification processes delay the launch because they are started too late
  • Quality problems only show up after launch, when returns rise
  • Responsibilities between supplier, brand and certifier are unclear

Frequently asked questions.

Does growald handle the certification itself?

No, the actual certification (e.g. CE) is carried out by specialized institutes; growald coordinates the process.

Is this legal advice?

No, growald does not provide legal advice. For legal assessments we recommend a specialized lawyer or the certification institute.

How long does a CE certification typically take?

It depends heavily on the product, often several weeks, so an early start pays off.

What happens if a sample does not meet the criteria?

It is re-requested with documentation, or the supplier is changed, before the series order is placed.

Does this also apply to existing products that are already listed?

Yes, a retroactive check is just as possible as one before a new launch.
