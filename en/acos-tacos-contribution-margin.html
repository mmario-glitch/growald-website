Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS?

ACoS only relates to advertised revenue, TACoS to total revenue. TACoS shows the fuller picture.

How is break-even ACoS calculated?

From margin, fixed costs and target profit per product: individually, not as a flat figure.

Is the standard Amazon report enough for this?

No, it needs to be linked to your contribution margin calculation.

How often is the control logic adjusted?

When prices, costs or margins change, and at least quarterly.

Does this also work for new products without history?

Yes, with an estimated target ACoS that is calibrated after the first data comes in.
