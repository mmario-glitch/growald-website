Keyword, market & competitor analysis

Understand what customers actually search for on Amazon, and how competitors are currently positioned.

When keywords are guessed instead of researched.

Many listings are based on assumptions rather than real search data.

  • Backend keywords and titles are based on assumptions rather than search data
  • Competitor listings are not analyzed systematically
  • Search term opportunities (niches with demand but little competition) go undiscovered
  • The keyword strategy is created once and never updated
  • PPC and SEO keywords are treated separately instead of together

Frequently asked questions.

Which tools are used for the analysis?

Standard Amazon keyword and analysis tools, supplemented by manual market observation.

How many keywords result?

Depending on the category, usually 50 to 200 relevant keywords, prioritized by opportunity.

Is international search taken into account?

Yes, on request, especially when internationalization is planned.

How often should the analysis be updated?

At least annually, or when there are major changes to the assortment or market.

Does this replace a trademark check of the terms?

No, growald does not provide legal advice. For trademark law questions, we recommend a specialized lawyer.
