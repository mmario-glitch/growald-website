Assess product by product whether Vendor Central or Seller Central is likely to bring the higher profit, without a blanket recommendation.

Frequently asked questions.

Is Seller Central generally better than Vendor Central?

No, that depends on the product, the category and the individual terms. Blanket statements are not credible.

Can both models be used in parallel?

In certain cases yes, and that is assessed product by product.

How long does a model switch take?

That depends heavily on Amazon-internal processes and is outside our direct control.

Is the contract with Amazon reviewed too?

No, growald does not offer legal advice. We recommend a specialized lawyer for the contract review.

What if the analysis does not give a clear result?

Then we say so transparently, along with the factors that speak for each option.
