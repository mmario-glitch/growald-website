Workflow automation for businesses

Automate recurring routine tasks instead of copying data between systems by hand every day.

Where routine tasks cost time today.

In many companies, employees copy data back and forth between Excel, email and systems every day.

  • Recurring routine tasks tie up capacity that is needed for value-adding work
  • No error control for manual data transfers
  • Processes are not documented, so knowledge depends on individual people
  • In-house IT resources to build automation yourself are missing
  • Small errors in routine processes stay undetected for a long time

Automated stock and order reconciliation

Orders, stock levels and prices have to stay consistent between the online shop, Amazon Seller Central and ERP/inventory management. We automate this reconciliation instead of maintaining it by hand in Excel, with error notification when values differ.

Result: Routine tasks run reliably in the background, errors are caught early, and the team gains time for tasks that actually need decisions.
