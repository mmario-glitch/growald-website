Process analysis & automation potential

Identify recurring manual work in everyday e-commerce operations and automate it where it counts.

Where time is lost in daily operations.

Many teams lose time on processes that could actually be automated.

  • Order processing, reporting or customer service tie up far too much manual time
  • Data is transferred by hand between systems several times
  • The risk of errors grows with the number of manual steps
  • There is no overview of which processes can be automated at all
  • Automation ideas stay wishful thinking without concrete implementation planning
